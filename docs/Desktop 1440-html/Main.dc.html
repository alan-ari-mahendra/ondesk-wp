012 / FAQ

faq

Straight answers to the questions owners ask before they switch on an assistant.

001Will it sound like a robot?
No. You set the tone and approve example answers. Most customers can't tell, and Ondesk always tells people it's an assistant if asked.
002What if it doesn't know the answer?
It never guesses prices or medical advice. It tells the customer a team member will reply and flags the chat for you.
003Which calendars and booking tools does it work with?
Google Calendar, Calendly, Fresha, Mindbody and Square Appointments. More are added every month.
004How long does setup take?
Most businesses are live in one afternoon. Our team can set it up for you on Growth and Pro.
005Is my customer data safe?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the US or EU, and never used to train public models.
006Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no setup fees. Export your conversations whenever you like.
